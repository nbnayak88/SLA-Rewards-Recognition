# Certificate and Verification Specification

> Official mission (exact wording on **every award**): **Architecting Experiences for a Better World**.

## Required certificate fields
- SUCCESSLABS ACADEMY
- ARCHITECTURE EXCELLENCE & CAREER IMPACT AWARDS
- CERTIFICATE OF RECOGNITION (Levels 1–6) or CERTIFICATE OF DISTINGUISHED RECOGNITION (Legacy)
- Full award title and approved recipient display name
- Award-specific recognition paragraph from its charter
- Validated pathway, engagement title/outcome summary and **real/simulated label where materially relevant**
- Award ID, issue date, authorized signatory and verification reference
- Mission footer exactly as shown above

## Design guidance
Strategic blue `#1E3A8A`; precision silver gray `#D1D5DB`; applied gold `#F59E0B`; white `#FFFFFF`. Maintain accessible contrast, minimal typography and an Academy-approved font (Comfortaa may be used where brand-approved). Logo top-right when available. Do not redistribute proprietary font files.

## Status and issuance
Only issue certificates after documented human approval. Track proposed → under_review → approved → issued or revoked. QR codes may be generated **only** after a controlled verification endpoint is implemented. Do not show an unapproved award as valid. Store private evidence outside GitHub.

For complete unique wording, see each [award charter](../README.md).
