# Success Pathfinder Award — SLA-RNR-002

> **Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.  
> **Policy:** Proposed v1.0, November 2026. **Recognition:** I Progressed. **Passing mark:** 75/100.

## Purpose
Recognize **meaningful, sustained professional progression beyond a first breakthrough**. The award distinguishes new capability and achievement from repeating an introductory program.

## Qualifying pathways
| Code | Pathway | Evidence of progression |
|---|---|---|
| SP-01 | Career Transition | Verified advanced role or new functional discipline |
| SP-02 | Career Acceleration | Increased responsibility and professional outcome |
| SP-03 | Architecture Specialization | Advanced applied specialization |
| SP-04 | Advanced Bootcamp | Second **distinct** Bootcamp plus substantially advanced validated output |
| SP-05 | Advanced 90-Day Hackathon | Further independently validated solution, complexity or leadership |
| SP-06 | Enterprise Project Progression | More demanding project role and deliverable |
| SP-07 | Consulting Practice Development | Credible progression in consulting scope |
| SP-08 | Community Contribution | Additional independently verified contribution |

## Eligibility and gates
- Breakthrough Award or independently verified equivalent first milestone.
- **At least two dated progression checkpoints** plus the baseline; intermediate evidence strongly preferred.
- Demonstrated increased ability, responsibility, complexity or result beyond the first achievement.
- Independent endorsement, applied artifact, transparent individual contribution, integrity and privacy checks.
- All career stages and membership tiers eligible.

## Lifecycle checkpoints
1. Establish the baseline and next professional ambition.
2. Develop a plan with an observable growth outcome.
3. Apply learning and capture progress and artifacts.
4. Demonstrate a subsequent validated milestone.

## Evidence dossier
- Prior milestone and verified baseline.
- Dated progression plan and checkpoint log.
- Applied project artifact or employment/role evidence.
- Independent stakeholder/facilitator verification.
- 200–400-word reflective statement.
- Confidentiality, AI-authorship and optional publication declarations.

## Assessment process
Assess progress relative to the nominee's circumstances rather than prestige of employer or absolute salary. Independent reviewer confirms the two checkpoints, outcome and contribution. Target ten business days after evidence completion; appeals within 30 days. One award per member. Award ID **SLA-SP-YYYY-0001**.
