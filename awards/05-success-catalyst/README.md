# Success Catalyst Award — SLA-RNR-005

> **Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.  
> **Policy:** Proposed v1.0, November 2026. **Recognition:** I Transformed. **Passing mark:** 88/100.

## Purpose
Recognize **substantial and independently verified transformation** of a career, business, enterprise, institution or community. Catalyst evaluates *what changed*, *how the nominee contributed* and *how the outcome was validated*. It does not require a formal architect job title.

| SC-12 | Learning-to-Impact Transformation | Bootcamp / Hackathon outcomes deployed or adopted with real verified value |

## Eligibility and gates
- Prior Architect or independently verified **equivalent transformation capability**; non-architect pathways are fully valid.
- One meaningful **completed** result, not a forecast or announcement.
- Four checkpoints: **Establish Baseline → Execute Transformation → Validate Impact → Confirm Sustainability**.
- Credible measurement method, attribution and independent verification.
- Compensation growth of **30%** is an *indicative benchmark only*, never automatic approval; below 30% can qualify with compelling wider change. Do not publish private salary details.
- A normal 24-month window for the achievement is proposed, with justified exceptions.

## Evidence dossier
1. Baseline, actual outcome and measurement periods.
2. Transformation objective, constraints, methods and key decisions.
3. Demonstrable personal contribution, including collaborative attribution.
4. Before/after performance, role, beneficiary or business evidence.
5. Independent employer, sponsor, customer or reviewer attestation.
6. Sustained use/adoption or ongoing professional performance.
7. 300–500 word reflection and professional integrity declaration.
8. Private evidence IDs; separately consented public impact summary.

## Defense and governance
Thirty-minute impact defense covering baseline, actions, actual results, attribution and sustainability. Independent business/career/impact assessors. Target twenty business days after complete submission; thirty-day appeal window. One Catalyst Award per member. Award ID **SLA-SC-YYYY-0001**.
