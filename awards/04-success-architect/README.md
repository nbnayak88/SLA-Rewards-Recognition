# Success Architect Award — SLA-RNR-004

> **Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.  
> **Policy:** Proposed v1.0, November 2026. **Recognition:** I Architected and Owned. **Passing mark:** 85/100.

## Purpose
Recognize substantive ownership of **architecture decisions, integration, cross-domain complexity, stakeholder trade-offs and governance**. An Architect explains *why* a solution is right for an enterprise and owns relevant decisions, not merely its delivery.

## Eligibility and hard gates
- Prior Practitioner or independently verified equivalent.
- Own substantive decisions across **at least two materially connected architecture concerns**.
- **At least three reviewable architecture decision records (ADRs)**, plus two credible alternatives analyzed for a significant decision.
- Four dated checkpoints and independently validated architecture review.
- No automatic eligibility for senior job title, tool use, Bootcamp completion or generic diagrams.
- All career stages can qualify; simulated architecture is permitted only with realistic constraints, an independent defense and accurate labeling.

## Four architecture checkpoints
1. **Discover & Frame:** drivers, stakeholders, baseline, scope and constraints.
2. **Architect & Decide:** target state, design options, ADRs, decision trade-offs and traceability.
3. **Govern & Align:** risks, architecture principles, dependencies, reviews and transition approach.
4. **Validate & Evolve:** independent architecture review, feasibility, conformance and evolution roadmap.

## Evidence dossier
1. Context and current-state architecture.
2. Target-state models and interface/domain relationships.
3. Three or more substantive ADRs identifying alternatives, criteria, rationale, consequences.
4. Traceability from business outcomes to architectural decisions.
5. Governance record, risks, dependencies and transition road map.
6. Independent review acceptance and validated outcome or feasibility evidence.
7. Individual ownership and contribution statement.
8. 25–30-minute architecture defense and integrity declarations.

## Review Board
