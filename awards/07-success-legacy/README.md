## Eligibility and hard gates
- Vanguard Award or independently verified equivalent multiplier leadership capability.
- At least one demonstrable substantial enduring contribution with evidence of **continuity beyond direct control**.
- **At least three distinct independent beneficiaries or successor groups** with validated outcomes.
- Documented transfer of knowledge, governance, decision rights or stewardship.
- Five lifecycle checkpoints completed.
- Normally 24 months of documented creation, adoption and continuity; exceptionally compelling equivalent evidence may justify an exception without confusing promises with actual sustained results.
- No requirement for age, retirement, fame, wealth, title or seniority. Independent Council validation and ethical-integrity clearance required.

## Five checkpoints
1. **Originate:** purpose, baseline and contribution history.
2. **Institutionalize:** methods, governance, knowledge, resources, operational adoption.
3. **Transfer:** successors become independently competent and own decisions.
4. **Sustain:** verified outcomes continue beyond nominee's day-to-day presence.
5. **Evolve:** successor-led improvement and adaptation are demonstrated.

## Required Legacy Evidence Dossier
1. 500–750-word Legacy statement with enduring contribution and beneficiaries.
2. Historical baseline and dated milestone timeline.
3. Institutionalized framework, learning system, practice, publication or governance artifacts.
4. Transfer records, successor readiness and actual stewardship.
5. Continuity measures and sustained beneficiary outcomes.
6. Evidence of successor-led improvements and preservation.
7. At least **three independent perspectives**, including at least one successor/current steward.
8. Ethical impact, accessibility, responsible innovation and unintended consequences.
9. Continuity and preservation plan, integrity and AI assistance statements; separate public consent.
10. Evidence classification; no sensitive beneficiary or employer details in public repository.

## Legacy impact chain
**Created → Adopted → Transferred → Sustained → Evolved.** The strongest Legacy evidence shows a living contribution that successors can maintain and improve, not just a historically popular artifact.

## Independent Council review
Five independent experts spanning transformation, learning, leadership and succession, impact, and ethics. Recommended **60-minute Legacy defense** with successor testimony and sources not authored solely by nominee. Document founder/sponsor conflicts and recusal. Target forty-five business days; thirty-day appeal. One Legacy Award per member, conferred annually only when standard is met. Award ID **SLA-SL-YYYY-0001**.
