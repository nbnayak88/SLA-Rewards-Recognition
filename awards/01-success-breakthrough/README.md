# Success Breakthrough Award — SLA-RNR-001

> **Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.  
> **Policy:** Proposed v1.1, November 2026. **Recognition:** I Finished. **Passing mark:** 70/100.

## Purpose
The inaugural SuccessLabs Champions recognition for a verified first professional or applied-learning achievement. It explicitly includes successful completion of **one full Bootcamp** or **one 90-Day Hackathon** with assessed outcomes, regardless of whether an employment outcome occurs.

## Qualifying pathways
| Code | Qualifying pathway | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|
| SB-00 | Complete Bootcamp | All mandatory learning/practice, assignments and facilitator-assessed final deliverable |
| SB-00H | Complete 90-Day Hackathon | All mandatory milestones, validated working solution or transformation artifact, final defense |
| SB-01 | First Job | Verified first professional appointment |
| SB-02 | Internship | Verified applied internship and outcome |
| SB-03 | First Consulting Engagement | Independently accepted consulting deliverable |
| SB-04 | First Architecture Deliverable | Reviewed first architecture artifact |
| SB-05 | Career Transition | Verified meaningful first transition |
| SB-06 | Workplace Improvement | Credibly validated first improvement |
| SB-07 | Applied Capstone | Independently validated first practical capstone |

## Eligibility and hard gates
- Open to members from **all career stages and all membership tiers**; no pay-to-win.
- One complete achievement, not enrollment, attendance or a purchased certificate.
- SB-00: 100% of required activities and deliverables; assessment of a completed final artifact.
- SB-00H: complete mandatory 90-day progression; final defense; winning the competition is **not** necessary.
- Actual first-job/career/employer claims require real independent verification; a simulated capstone is explicitly labeled simulated.
- Credible evidence, original contribution, integrity declaration and publication consent decisions.

## Four checkpoints
1. Define baseline and first achievement.
2. Carry out required work, assignments or participation.
3. Produce final assessed outcome.
4. Secure independent validation and reflect.

## Evidence dossier
1. Baseline and 150–300-word achievement statement.
2. Primary evidence: final capstone, accepted artifact or redacted employment/engagement proof.
3. Completion record or dated milestone log where relevant.
4. Facilitator, employer, sponsor or assessor attestation.
5. Reflection (150–300 words) on learning, challenges and next steps.
6. Integrity, AI-assistance and confidentiality disclosures; separate public-consent option.

## Assessment and governance
Independent facilitator or panel review; target ten business days after complete dossier. One award per member, later achievements receive badges. Appeals permitted within 30 days. Award ID **SLA-SB-YYYY-0001**.
