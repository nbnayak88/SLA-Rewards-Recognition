# Success Vanguard Award — SLA-RNR-006

> **Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.  
> **Policy:** Proposed v1.0, November 2026. **Recognition:** I Multiplied Impact. **Passing mark:** 90/100.

## Purpose
Recognize individuals who **develop others and build repeatable enabling systems** so beneficiaries can deliver independently. Reach, follower counts, attendance and mentoring hours alone are not multiplier outcomes.

## Eligibility and hard gates
- Catalyst recognition or independently validated equivalent leadership/transformation competence.
- At least **two distinct, independently verified beneficiary or team outcomes**.
- A documented, reusable practice, framework, learning system or capability.
- Demonstrable **beneficiary autonomy** and relevant contribution by nominee.
- All four leadership checkpoints passed; independent verification and ethics gate.
- No managerial job title is required. A 6-month outcome window is a suggested evidence benchmark, not rigid tenure.

## Lifecycle checkpoints
1. **Define Collective Ambition:** beneficiary baseline, shared problem, objectives.
2. **Build the Enabling System:** repeatable method, governance, mentoring and reusable resources.
3. **Enable Independent Delivery:** beneficiaries demonstrate competence and produce outcomes without constant assistance.
4. **Validate Multiplied Impact:** independent outcomes, repeatability, ongoing adoption and autonomy.

## Leadership evidence dossier
1. Leadership charter with beneficiaries and baseline.
2. Reusable playbook, operating model, standards or learner-development architecture.
3. Independently validated outcomes for at least two distinct beneficiaries/teams.
4. Documented nominee contribution, coaching and capability transfer.
5. Dated evidence of autonomous decisions or outputs by others.
6. Repeatable use or replicated successful cohorts/teams.
7. Independent stakeholder and beneficiary testimony.
8. 400–600-word ethical leadership reflection, AI use disclosure and separate publication consent.

## Multiplier measurement
Measure **reach → capability → autonomy → independently verified impact**. Good indicators include independent project delivery, leaders developed, solution adoption, standards reused and continued competence without intervention.

## Review and governance
Three independent assessors spanning leadership, transformation and evaluation. Recommended 40-minute defense: problem, system, beneficiary outcomes, autonomy, sustainability and challenge questions. Target thirty business days after complete evidence; thirty-day appeal window. One Vanguard Award per member. Award ID **SLA-SV-YYYY-0001**.
