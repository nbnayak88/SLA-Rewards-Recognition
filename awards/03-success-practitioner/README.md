# Success Practitioner Award — SLA-RNR-003

> **Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.  
> **Policy:** Proposed v1.0, November 2026. **Recognition:** I Delivered. **Passing mark:** 80/100.

## Purpose
Recognize a professional who has **independently demonstrated complete end-to-end delivery** of a meaningful solution, with validation. Rigorous simulated case work is eligible only when transparently labeled and independently panel-assessed; an accepted advisory solution may qualify without a production deployment where the defined scope is advisory.

## Eligibility
- Pathfinder or independently assessed equivalent.
- One completed substantive solution, not a diagram collection, copied template or course certificate.
- **Three dated checkpoints:** Discover & Design → Build & Deliver → Validate & Assure.
- Independent acceptance or panel validation, reproducible evidence, and personal contribution.
- Transparent classification as production, advisory or simulated engagement.

## Evidence dossier
1. Baseline, engagement charter, problem and stakeholders.
2. Scope, requirements, design and rationale.
3. Versioned working solution, artifacts, tests or accepted advice.
4. Dated delivery checkpoint history and traceability.
5. Accepted outcome or independent panel review.
6. Contribution and AI-assistance statements.
7. 15–20-minute practitioner defense record (typically 10–12 minutes presentation plus questions).
8. Integrity, confidentiality and publication-consent decisions.
