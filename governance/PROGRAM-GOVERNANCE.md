# SuccessLabs Champions — Program Governance

> Mission: **Architecting Experiences for a Better World**.  
> Draft policy v1.0, November 2026.

## Foundational rules
1. All career stages and all membership tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Quantum Circle) are eligible without preference.
2. Recognition is never purchasable; attendance, enrollment, credentials, role titles and social visibility alone cannot qualify.
3. Prior award or independent equivalence may establish prerequisite capability. Each level has separate outcome requirements.
4. Live work and rigorous simulation are different evidence categories and must be labeled accurately.
5. Each of seven milestone awards is normally issued once per member. Additional achievements may receive **badges**, not duplicate awards.
6. Members can decline public recognition without penalty.
7. All reviewers disclose conflicts of interest; founder, sponsor, employer or donor status cannot change the standard.
8. Version the policies and apply the disclosed version effective at nomination intake.
9. Written appeals within 30 days are reviewed by people independent of the original decision.
10. Revocation needs documented investigation, notice and an opportunity to respond.

All passing scores are subject to **dimension minima, independent verification and hard gates**.

## Proposed cadence
Monthly: Breakthrough and Pathfinder. Quarterly: Practitioner, Architect, Catalyst. Quarterly or semiannually: Vanguard. Annually: Legacy. Intake may remain open throughout the year.

## Award lifecycle
Nominate → establish identity and eligibility → evaluate confidential dossier → independently score and defend → moderate → approve/defer/decline → register award → obtain separate publication consent → issue certificate / QR verification → publish optional story → support corrections, appeals and revocation.

## Distinguish awards, badges and qualifications
- Seven *milestone awards* mark increasing maturity and responsibility.
- *Achievement badges* identify outcomes such as Career Reinvention, Certification Excellence, Architecture Excellence, Transformation Impact and Mentorship Excellence.
- *Professional certifications* are external or separately governed and must not be implied by these awards.

## Scope and taxonomy
12 architecture streams × 11 GICS sectors; seven learning domains (HR, CX, Finance, Procurement, SCM, BTM, Leadership & Identity). Origin Story → Pre-Foundation → Foundation → Essentials → Advanced → Ultimate is a **learning arc**, not seven award levels.

## Operating note
This repository documents proposed policy. It does not create a private evidence system, member records, an approved board, secure verification endpoint or issued certificates.
