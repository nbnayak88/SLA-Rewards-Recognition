# Evidence, Privacy and Integrity Policy

> Mission: **Architecting Experiences for a Better World**.  
> Draft v1.0 — do not store candidate-sensitive evidence in this public repository.

## Evidence provenance
For every material artifact record reference ID, type, date, creator, scope, provenance, version, confidentiality classification, reviewer and validation. Dossiers connect **baseline → action → nominee contribution → actual outcome → independent verification**.

## Verification hierarchy
1. Independent employer/client/sponsor/recipient confirmation, actual performance evidence and formally accepted deliverables.
2. Auditable original artifacts: versioned GitHub work, ADRs, tests, portfolios and dated plans.
3. Independent panel-validated simulation or recorded defense that clearly identifies simulated scope.
4. Self-report for context only, never sole proof of impact.

## Simulations
A validated simulation may demonstrate professional capability for the relevant award, but fictional enterprises and estimated benefits **must not be presented as real client implementations, employment outcomes, salaries or deployed transformations**. Use explicit labels in scorecards and certificates.

## Confidentiality
- Public: policy, anonymized examples, consented accomplishment summaries and minimal verifier records.
- Private: names or contact details lacking consent, compensation records, full resumes, client contracts, beneficiary identities, individual rubrics, confidential evidence and review notes.
- Enforce least-privilege access, audit trails and retention limits in the eventual private system.
- Recipient and third-party permissions are distinct. Refusing public recognition must not harm assessment.

## AI-assisted evidence
Declare AI support; explain sources, human decisions and individual contribution. Require a defense that distinguishes independently understood work from copied or fabricated outputs. Unattributed plagiarism, fake endorsements, fabricated metrics and material privacy violations are disqualifying.

## Measurement and attribution
Use dated before/after measures with method, attribution and contextual factors. Distinguish reach, attendance, competency, autonomy, adoption and impact. Do not monetize outcomes without a supportable model.

## Verification privacy
QR codes should link only to a secure status page that displays recipient-approved name, award type, date and status. Do not expose raw evidentiary files or private pay details. Build a retention/deletion policy before live intake.
