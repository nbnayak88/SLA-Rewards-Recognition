# SuccessLabs — Assessor Handbook

> Mission: **Architecting Experiences for a Better World**. Proposed v1.0.

## Assessment sequence
1. Record award, pathway, policy version, baseline and real/simulated classification.
2. Verify mandatory prerequisites and dated checkpoints; request missing evidence.
3. Allocate conflict-free reviewers competent in the domain.
4. Score **each rubric dimension independently** with supporting artifact IDs and reasons.
5. Verify overall threshold, every dimension minimum, integrity and all mandatory gates.
6. Conduct the award's defense and independently validate important external claims.
7. Record approve / defer / decline. Send specific feedback.
8. Escalate appeals to an independent reviewer within the defined 30-day filing window.

## Scoring anchors
| Share of dimension | Performance |
|---|---|
| 90–100% | Exceptional |
| 80–89% | Strong |
| 65–79% | Satisfactory, often below tier distinction |
| 40–64% | Developing |
| <40% | Insufficient |

The panel must not waive hard gates because the total score is high. Panels should challenge decision ownership, methods, alternatives, attribution, beneficiary autonomy and continuity according to tier.

## Review independence
A reviewer must declare personal/financial conflicts; conflicted individuals recuse themselves. For Vanguard use a three-person multidisciplinary leadership panel; for Legacy a five-person independent council including impact and ethics expertise.

## Pilot and calibration
Test score consistency across early-career through senior professionals, 11 sectors, functional vs architecture roles and real vs simulated cases. Document any changes in a versioned policy and avoid retroactive undisclosed rules.
